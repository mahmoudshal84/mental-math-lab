Boss drawings

Give any boss a student-drawn look. Scan the drawing (or take a photo in good light) and upload up to 3 poses. The poses play in a loop, so a hand can wave or a mouth can chomp. The drawing is used in every battle against that boss and on every student's trophy shelf.

Tips for students: draw on plain white paper with dark outlines and bold colors, and fill most of the page. For poses 2 and 3, trace pose 1 onto a new sheet (hold both up to a window) and change only the part that moves.
