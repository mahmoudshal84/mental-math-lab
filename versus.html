  • Race to 5 Both teams get the same board, 5 problems per player. Type an answer and press Enter to clear the problem it matches. First team to clear its board wins the round. Best of 3.
  • Blast Battle 20 problems fall for everyone at the same time. The first player to type the answer and press Enter blasts it and gets the point. Most points wins.
  • Don't guess An answer that doesn't match anything freezes your typing for a moment.
  • Coins and your record 1 coin for every problem you clear or blast. Wins and losses go on your record, and leaving a match early counts as a loss.
